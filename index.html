Straight answers

Everything people ask us before their first order.

How fast is delivery?

CBD: 25–35 min. Westlands, Kilimani, Upperhill, Parklands: 35–45 min. We text you the moment the rider leaves.

What's the delivery fee?

KES 100 in CBD. KES 150 to outer zones. Free over KES 1,500. The fee is confirmed before you pay.

Do you take M-Pesa?

Yes. We send you the Till number on WhatsApp when you confirm your order. Cash and card on delivery also work.

Is there a minimum order?

KES 300. Below that, the standard delivery fee applies.

Is the meat really organic?

Yes — one farm in Kiambu, the same one for two years. Ask us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you the name.

What if my food arrives cold?

Send us a photo within 15 minutes. We remake it or refund you. No argument.

How spicy is "Pili Pili Proper"?

Proper. If you're unsure, start with Medium — you can always add more from the sachet we send with every meat order.

Can I order for a group?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with the headcount and time. For 6+ people we'll confirm a pickup or delivery window in writing.
