Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly are you located in Imara?

We're on the ground floor of Imara Daima Business Park, just off Mombasa Road. Dedicated, well-lit, 24/7 guarded parking is available for all gym members.

How do payments work? Do you accept M-Pesa?

Yes — instant M-Pesa (Till/Paybill) at the front desk or via WhatsApp booking. Cash and major cards are also accepted.

I am a total beginner. Will I get support?

100%. Every new member gets an introductory session with a certified coach covering equipment, safety, and posture.

Is steam room and sauna access included?

Yes — all Monthly Beast and Quarterly Champion packages include steam room and detox sauna access.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes. Monthly memberships can be frozen for up to 14 days with prior WhatsApp notice.
